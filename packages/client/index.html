Controls

Keyboard: ← → move, ↓ drop faster, ↑ or X turn right, Z turn left, Esc or P pause.

Mouse: the piece follows the pointer. Left click or wheel turns, hold right button to drop.

Gamepad or joystick: d-pad or stick moves and drops, A / Y turn right, B / X turn left, Start pauses.

Touch: the buttons under the board (pick a layout under Touch buttons). Or drag on the board to slide the piece, drag down to drop it faster, tap to turn right, flick up to turn left.

Your game saves every time a piece lands and whenever you switch apps. Signed in, it follows you to your other devices.
