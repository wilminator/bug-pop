How to play

Pop every bug

Bugs sit on the board. Pieces fall from the top, two halves at a time. Steer and turn each piece as it falls. Clear every bug to win the level.

Line up four or more of one color, across or up and down. Bugs and piece halves both count, and everything in the line pops.

→

Pieces left hanging in the air fall until they land on something: a lone half drops straight down, and a whole piece drops once nothing holds up either half. Bugs never fall. Whatever falls can line up and pop again, which is a chain.

The game is over when the pile reaches the top and a new piece has no room to come in.

The colors

Each color has its own shape, so you can tell them apart without color. Turn on Shapes on pieces in Settings to put the shapes on the pieces too.

    You pick 2 to 5 colors before each game. More colors is harder.

    Levels and speed

    Each level adds more bugs: 4 on level 0, then 4 more every 3 levels, up to a full board at level 60. Pieces also fall a little faster each level.

    SpeedPoints
    Slo-mo (4 or 5 colors only)×0.5
    Low×1
    Medium×2
    High×3

    The Level slider starts with levels 0 to 20 open. Clear a level to open the next one. Each speed keeps its own progress.

    Beat level 100 to open Endless at that speed. In Endless the board never resets: when you clear the bugs, a new wave crawls in around whatever is left, and your score keeps going.

    Scoring

    Every bug you pop scores 100 × chain bonus × multiplier × the speed's points.

    • Chain bonus: the more bugs one piece pops, the more each is worth. The first bug is ×1, the next ×2, then ×4, and so on up to ×32. Bugs popped by halves falling afterwards count for the same piece. It starts over with every new piece.
    • Multiplier: starts at ×1.0 each level and shows under your score. Each bug you pop raises it by 0.2. Each new piece lowers it by 0.1, but never below ×1.0.

    So pop several bugs with one piece, and keep popping without wasting pieces, to keep both climbing.

    Clean sweep: finish a level with no piece halves left on the board for a bonus of 1,000 + 100 per level, times the speed's points (level 10 on Medium: 4,000).

    Pausing and saving

    Your game saves every time a piece lands and whenever you switch apps, so you can come back to it any time. Pausing hides the board.
